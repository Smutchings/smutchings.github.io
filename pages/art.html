Art

The expression of emotional content.

Art is the antithesis of Design. There is no problem to solve or solution to find. Art can, and often is, be pursued purely in an attempt to create something.

The pieces of work shown here had no problem to solve. They are not in search of a solution. They are purely creative exploration for the sake of exploration.
