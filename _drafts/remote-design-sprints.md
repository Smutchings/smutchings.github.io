# Remote Design Sprints

In just two weeks, a Remote Design Sprint helps you and your business:

- Identify a key challenge you're facing
- Come up with diverse ideas on how to face the challenge
- Decide on an idea to take forward to prototyping
- Test this prototype with real customers and users
